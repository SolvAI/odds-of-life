## 1969 · birth: Born in the Mud, Baptized in Rainwater
September 1969, and the Hong Kong flu is doing laps through the barrios of Iloilo. You arrive early, small, and blue around the mouth, in a shack that smells of wet nipa and fish paste. The midwife holds you up by the heels like a caught catfish and says nothing. Outside, the carabao stands in the rain, and your father is already out there trying to sell it. Your mother names you Maria after every saint she can afford to pray to, which is one.

## 1979 · age 10 · The Copra Shed
`Sent out to work`
The money men come on a Tuesday, so Efren sends you to Don Ricardo's copra shed to sort nuts for fifty centavos a day. You sit on a burlap sack splitting shells with a dull bolo, and the dust makes your chest ache by noon. The other children are faster and stronger, but you count the nuts in piles of ten and Don Ricardo notices. He says a child who can count can weigh the sacks next harvest, and you keep the coin tied in your handkerchief.
