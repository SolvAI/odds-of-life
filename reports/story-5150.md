## 1978 · birth: Born Under the Red Sky
1978, Addis Ababa, in the second year of the Derg and the year they shot the streets into line. You arrive small and wheezing in a rented room in Mercato, three flights up, over a shop that sells knock-off batteries and powdered milk. Your mother names you Tesfaye — the one who brings hope — because the family has run out of everything else. The curfew siren starts before your first cry settles. Outside, a truck idles with its lights off, and the men in it are writing names on a list, not yours but someone's, and your father keeps the window shut anyway.
